SLSRE Dissolution — Alert Routing Migration Plan

Date: 2026-11-05
Scope: PKO/ADO, managed-tenants, ACM/OCM, and other addon alerts
Out of scope: RHOAM per-cluster alerts (handled by RHOAM team via MGDAPI-7107)

TL;DR: Most SLSRE-paged alerts (OSD FM, Hive, HyperShift) already route to the SREP pager — no change needed. Three areas require action: (1) ACM alerts on the SLSRE escalation policy (1,159 incidents/yr, currently silent), (2) addon-operator and package-operator alerts on RHOBS, and (3) cleanup of orphaned PD receivers and app-interface config.

1. ACM Alerts — Service Lifecycle On-Call (PGASVAO)

Risk: 1,159 ACM alerts hit this EP in the last year. They went silent in Sep 2026 — if they recur after SLSRE dissolution, there will be no one on the pager.

The SLSRE escalation policy (PGASVAO — "Service Lifecycle On-Call") has no alertmanager routes pointing to it, yet its attached PD service (Service Lifecycle SRE) received 1,159 incidents in the past year. All are ACM-related alerts originating from management clusters (likely via RHOBS or a direct PD integration, not the centralized alertmanager).
